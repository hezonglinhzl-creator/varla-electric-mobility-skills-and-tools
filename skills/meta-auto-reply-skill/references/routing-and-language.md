# Varla Meta routing and reply language

## Reply examples

### KOL, 8k or higher

"Thanks for your interest in working with Varla. Please send your profile links, follower count, average views, audience countries, engagement data, and a few recent relevant posts. Our team will review the fit before confirming any collaboration."

### KOL, below 8k or no performance data

"Thanks for reaching out. We are not able to offer a free vehicle at this stage, but you can join the community through the link in our bio/Linktree and ask an admin about available closed-beta pricing."

### Pre-sales / order fallback

"For the latest purchase, order, shipping, or tracking information, please contact our pre-sales team at support@varlascooter.com."

### After-sales fallback

"For warranty, repair, refund, or product-support help, please contact our after-sales team at customer@varlascooter.com so they can review your case accurately."

## Do not send

- More than one reply to one inbound message.
- A reply during the 15-minute human handoff pause.
- Repeated generic acknowledgements.
- A response to `ok`, `thanks`, emoji-only text, or another low-information acknowledgement without a new actionable intent.
- Unverified price, stock, tracking, warranty outcome, commission, free-product eligibility, or delivery date.
