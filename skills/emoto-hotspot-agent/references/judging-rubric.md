# eMoto Hotspot Judging Rubric

Use this rubric after collecting candidate trends.

## Candidate Fields

Capture these fields for each hotspot before judging:

- `hotspot_name`
- `source_platform`
- `format_type`
- `evidence_of_heat`
- `us_relevance`
- `natural_emoto_fit`
- `production_feasibility`
- `risk_flags`
- `best_channel`
- `recommended_action`

## Scoring Lens

Score each area from `0` to `3`.

### 1. Heat

- `3`: repeated across multiple fresh posts, strong engagement pattern, visibly rising or heavily discussed now
- `2`: clearly performing well but spread is still limited
- `1`: interesting signal but weak confirmation
- `0`: stale, isolated, or mostly assumed

### 2. US Relevance

- `3`: obviously US-native language, context, timing, or holiday
- `2`: global trend that translates cleanly to US audience behavior
- `1`: mixed geography or unclear US pull
- `0`: mainly outside US context

### 3. eMoto Fit

- `3`: natural electric dirt bike angle without forcing
- `2`: good format fit with light adaptation
- `1`: only partially usable
- `0`: basically unrelated

### 4. Shootability

- `3`: can be filmed today with current bike, rider, garage, trail, or lifestyle assets
- `2`: needs a small extra setup or creator help
- `1`: needs heavy planning or missing assets
- `0`: unrealistic with current resources

### 5. Risk

Risk is inverse-scored. Lower risk is better.

- `3`: low risk, normal brand-safe execution
- `2`: manageable with wording and framing adjustments
- `1`: high moderation, safety, or controversy concern
- `0`: should not be used

## Suggested Decision Logic

- `可做`: mostly `2` and `3`, with risk at `2` or `3`
- `可观察`: promising heat but weak confirmation or timing still forming
- `可改编`: format is useful, but direct topic fit is weak
- `跳过`: fit or feasibility too low
- `风险`: safety, illegality, minors, deceptive framing, or moderation concern

## Automatic Risk Flags

Move directly to `风险` if any of these appear:

- Illegal road riding angle
- Dangerous stunt imitation
- Underage high-speed riding
- Reckless behavior framed as easy or normal
- False range or speed implication
- Conflict-bait, outrage-bait, or harassment angle
- Copyright-sensitive BGM dependence without an alternative

## Best-Channel Heuristic

- `TikTok`: fast POV, sound-led, meme-led, surprise reaction, debate-heavy comments
- `Instagram`: cleaner visuals, aspirational garage or outdoor look, gift or lifestyle reveal
- `YouTube Shorts`: simple title logic, before/after, test, challenge, comparison
- `KOL`: when execution needs a stronger rider, personality, or creator trust layer

## Prioritization

- `P0`: strong do-now opportunity with clean fit and low risk
- `P1`: good opportunity, not as urgent
- `P2`: worth testing if resources allow
- `P3`: low-confidence or backup option
