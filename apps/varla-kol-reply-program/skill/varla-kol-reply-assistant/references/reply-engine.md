# Reply Engine

Use this reference when editing templates, prompt rules, stage routing, or creator-facing copy.

## Default Reply Structure

- Generate 6 replies total
- Group into:
  - Natural 2
  - Formal 2
  - Short 2

## Brand Rules

- Product exchange first
- Commission can be added for stronger creators if approved
- `M5S` is the normal first-stage creator bike
- `M7S` is for stronger, higher-fit, more performance-oriented creators
- Do not promise:
  - sponsorship
  - free bike
  - paid collaboration
  - guaranteed shipment
  - guaranteed refund
  - guaranteed cooperation

## Common Posting Rules

- Draft approval before posting
- Instagram collaborator when possible: `@varla_adventure`
- TikTok tag or mention: `@varla_sport`
- Approved posts should stay live unless Varla approves otherwise

## Content Direction Defaults

- Prefer authentic riding, POV, daily-use, outdoor, family/outdoor, talking plus riding
- Avoid hard-ad tone
- Keep unsupported product claims as unknown or pending
