# Seedance/Codex Benchmark Logic

This reference summarizes the provided tutorial-style benchmark video:

`codexseedance 复刻制作各种视频#TikTok #带货 #出海 #电商.mp4`

Use this as a reusable pattern when the user says to follow the same logic.

## Observed Structure

- Starts with a human trust anchor: a podcast/interview style speaker appears first, giving the video a real-person authority frame.
- Moves quickly into screen recording: the creator uploads benchmark material and product-related assets to an AI workspace.
- Uses visible subtitles with high-contrast yellow text to keep the tutorial moving even without perfect audio.
- Demonstrates a problem: AI-generated video can have small bugs, inconsistent frames, or unusable results if the prompt is too loose.
- Shows a workflow correction: ask the AI to first understand the benchmark structure before generating.
- Converts the benchmark into a shot-by-shot plan: timing, lens, action, camera style, product requirements, and restrictions.
- Replaces the original product with a new product by preserving the shot logic and changing the product identity.
- Ends with an output proof: generated prompt text and generated product video/image examples are shown as evidence.

## Transferable Formula

1. Upload benchmark video.
2. Upload product image or product information.
3. Ask AI to analyze the benchmark's storyboard before generating.
4. Extract: shot timing, camera movement, product position, actor action, subtitle/voiceover structure.
5. Ask AI to rewrite for the user's product.
6. Generate images/storyboard frames.
7. Verify visual consistency.
8. Feed selected images and shot prompts into a video generator such as Jimeng.
9. Export captions and posting copy for TikTok/Instagram.

## Useful Beat Map

| Beat | Function | Replacement Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Human opener | Trust and attention | Use a real-looking host or expert, not a generic avatar |
| Upload assets | Shows process | Replace with user's product photos and benchmark screenshots |
| First AI output | Demonstrates possibility | Show rough generated frames or mock storyboard |
| Bug/limitation | Builds credibility | Admit what can fail: hands, face, product geometry, identity drift |
| Prompt refinement | Teaches method | Add constraints, timing, negatives, product details |
| Final prompt | Gives actionable value | Output copy/prompt text users can paste into Jimeng |
| Final visual proof | Payoff | Show product close-up, hand demo, lifestyle use, CTA shot |

## Style Notes

- Screen recording can be part of the video when the content is educational.
- For a pure product ad, convert the same logic into invisible planning: the audience sees the final product story, not the workflow.
- Yellow bold subtitles are effective for tutorial pacing but should be adapted to brand style for ads.
- The benchmark's main value is its workflow structure, not its exact words or visuals.
